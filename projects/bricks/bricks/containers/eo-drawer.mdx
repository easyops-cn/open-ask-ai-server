### Open Event & Close Event

```yaml preview minHeight="320px"
- brick: eo-button
  properties:
    textContent: open
  events:
    click:
      - target: "#drawer"
        method: open
- brick: eo-drawer
  properties:
    customTitle: 抽屉标题
    id: "drawer"
    placement: left
    keyboard: true
  events:
    open:
      - action: message.success
        args:
          - Drawer Open
    close:
      - action: message.success
        args:
          - Drawer Close
  children:
    - brick: eo-button
      properties:
        textContent: 关闭弹窗
      events:
        click:
          - target: "#drawer"
            method: "close"
```
