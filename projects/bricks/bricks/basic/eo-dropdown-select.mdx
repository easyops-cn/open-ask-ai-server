## Examples

### Basic

```yaml preview minHeight="200px"
brick: eo-dropdown-select
properties:
  size: large
  defaultValue: shenzhen
  options:
    - label: Beijing
      value: beijing
    - label: Shenzhen
      value: shenzhen
    - label: Guangzhou
      value: guangzhou
```
